- **Sources queried:** LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor, ZipRecruiter, SimplyHired, CareersInFood, BioSpace, Dice, Jooble, company career pages
- **Date filter:** Listings with posting indicators from September–October 2026 prioritized; older aggregator listings included where the employer is actively hiring at this title
- **Exclusion filter applied:** Director, Vice President/VP, President, Chief (CSO, CTO, etc.), C-suite roles removed from all listings
- **Deduplication:** Same title + same company = one entry; Asahi Kasei upstream associate listed under both Biopharma and Food Science given dual relevance; Cogent Scientific fermentation listed under both for same reason
- **Listings by industry:** Biopharma (22) · Advanced Manufacturing (17) · Food Science/Ag-Tech (15) · Food Operations/Supply Chain (25) · Cannabis Science (7) · Compliance & Safety (15)
- **Total listings:** 101

> ⚠️ **Always verify:** Job listings change quickly. Confirm that each posting is still open on the employer's own careers page before applying. Pay figures from aggregator sites may differ from actual offers.
